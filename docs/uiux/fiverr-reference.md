# Fiverr Reference UI Notes

These screenshots are layout references only. Ruang Usaha Kita must keep its own UMKM, kreator, layanan digital, navy-teal brand, and Apple-like visual direction.

## Useful Patterns

- Marketplace catalog pages use compact top search, filter, and sort controls, then give most desktop space to a dense and consistent card grid.
- Catalog pages should not spend prime desktop grid space on a large right-side guidance panel.
- Guidance, FAQ, and tips work better below the catalog grid, above the grid as a compact helper strip, or as collapsible helper content.
- Creator and service detail pages use a two-column structure: main profile/content on the left and a sticky pricing/action panel on the right.
- Marketplace cards use a clear hierarchy: visual/profile area, concise title/niche, badges or metadata, price/progress details, and action area.
- Card action buttons align consistently at the bottom so grid rows feel professional.
- Header layout is compact, sticky, and aligned with the same content gutter as the page body.
- Page content uses controlled viewport padding. Avoid combining centered max-width wrappers with large horizontal padding in a way that doubles the left and right gutters.
- Footer, FAQ, reviews, and secondary educational content belong after the primary marketplace content.

## Application To Ruang Usaha Kita

- Keep desktop page padding around 100px, tablet around 32px, and mobile around 20px.
- Use dense but readable catalog grids: 4 columns only when cards have enough room, otherwise 3 desktop, 2 tablet, 1 mobile.
- Keep filters compact and collapsible on desktop, with a drawer/sheet pattern on mobile and tablet.
- Keep all copy in the digital service marketplace domain: UMKM, kreator, paket jasa, layanan digital, brief campaign, hasil konten, revisi, status pesanan, pembayaran, invoice, portofolio, and review.
